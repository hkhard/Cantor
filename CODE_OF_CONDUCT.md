# Code of conduct

Cantor is made for choirs: people of every level singing together. Treat
everyone in this project the same way.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to
  music.
- Criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Ask before sharing anyone's recordings, scores or personal details.

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer through the
repository's private reporting channel. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block people who break
the rules.
