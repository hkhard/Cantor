# ChoirBird on Google Play

Everything the Play Console asks for to publish ChoirBird for Android, in
English (default language) and Swedish. Character limits are Google Play's
and are checked by `tools/store/check_text.py --play` (run it after any
edit). Graphics: see "Graphics" at the end.

The Android app is the same app as on iPad and iPhone, but the listing only
names what works on Android today. Not on Android yet (so not in the text):
lock-screen controls and playing on with the screen off, the share sheet,
"Open in ChoirBird" from other apps, folder sync, M4A export (WAV works),
Bluetooth page-turn pedals (untested).

**Full description** (4000):

```
ChoirBird is a practice app for choir singers. Open the score your conductor handed out and ChoirBird plays every voice on its own line, so you can learn your part at home, on the bus or on the way to rehearsal.

YOUR OWN SCORE
• The original PDF, exactly as printed. Page and bar numbers match everyone else's.
• Or the notes re-engraved from MusicXML, with transposition and parts you can hide.
• The bar being played is marked on the page, and the pages turn by themselves.

EVERY VOICE ON ITS OWN
• Soprano, alto, tenor, bass and piano each get a line with its own instrument.
• My part: your voice louder, the others softer. Without me: sing along with everyone but you.
• Volume, mute and solo for each voice, and mixes you can save per piece.

MADE FOR REHEARSING
• Slow the tempo down without changing the pitch.
• Loop a tricky passage between two bars.
• Click, count-in and a pitch pipe for your first note.
• Tap a bar to start from there.

SCAN PAPER MUSIC
• Photograph a printed sheet with the camera: ChoirBird straightens the pages and keeps them as a PDF score.

READ THE NOTES FROM A PDF
• Only have a PDF or a scan? ChoirBird can read the notes from it, here on your phone or tablet, and then play every part. It takes about a minute per page and carries on while you do other things.

MARK YOUR MUSIC
• Pen, highlighter, text and stamps (breaths, dynamics, cues) right on the page, in layers you can switch.

ON STAGE
• Performance mode shows only the music, with a small bar for pages and the pieces of a setlist.
• Turn pages with a tap or a swipe like a book.
• Light, dark and sepia, for any light.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Import MusicXML, PDF and forScore files (.4sc and .4ss) from your phone, Downloads or Google Drive.
• Setlists, search, tags and ratings, and a backup of your whole library in one file.
• Export practice tracks for your part as WAV.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
ChoirBird works without an internet connection, has no account and collects no data. Your scores stay on your device.

Mozart's Lacrymosa is included so you can try everything straight away.

ChoirBird is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The source code of every version is linked in About ChoirBird.

For Android phones and tablets (Android 8 and later), in English and Swedish.
```

### Data safety

- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No.
- **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?**
  Not asked when nothing is collected.
- **Account creation:** the app has no accounts.
- Reasons this is true:
  - ChoirBird itself makes no network calls (the UI harness blocks the
    network and fails on any request).
  - Note recognition (homr) and the PDF renderer run on the device.
  - The camera scanner is Google's ML Kit document scanner, which runs in
    Google Play services on the device. Google states the document
    scanner processes the images on the device and does not send them to
    Google. Its own diagnostic data is Google's, under Google Play
    services; Play's guidance is that a library's data is declared by the
    app if the app's code sends it, which ChoirBird does not. Re-check this
    answer if Google changes the ML Kit data disclosure.
- Permissions in the manifest: none beyond what Tauri and ML Kit add
  (internet is declared by the WebView template and unused; camera is held
  by Google Play services' scanner activity, not by ChoirBird).

## Testing tracks and publishing

ChoirBird is published under the product owner's **personal** Play
developer account. Google's rule for personal accounts created after
13 November 2023: before production, a **closed test with at least 12
testers opted in for 14 days in a row**, then an application for
production access (Google reviews it, usually within 7 days).

1. **Internal testing** (up to 100 testers, no review, available within
   minutes): for the product owner's own devices. Upload the AAB here
   first.
2. **Closed testing** ("Alpha" track or a named one): add at least 12
   testers by Google account email (a Google Group or an email list),
   send them the opt-in link, and keep them opted in for 14 days.
   Choir members are the natural testers; each must install from the
   opt-in link with the Google account that was added.
3. After 14 days: Dashboard › Apply for production access (questions about
   the test). Then publish to production.

Upload: `git tag android-vX.Y.Z && bash tools/android.sh release` builds
the signed AAB (`app-universal-release.aab`); upload it in Play Console ›
Testing › Internal testing › Create new release. Play App Signing:
accept Google-managed app signing; the upload key stays in
`~/.android-keys/` (password in the macOS Keychain, item
`choirbird-android-upload`). **Back the upload key up** (a password
manager or an encrypted disk): without it, Google must reset the upload key
before the next update.
